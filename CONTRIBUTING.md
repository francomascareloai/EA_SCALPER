# Contributing

This repository is a public **MQL5 snapshot** published for study and educational engineering collaboration.

Before contributing, read:

- `TRADING_RESTRICTIONS.md`
- `LICENSE`

## Scope of contributions (PR scope)

We accept PRs with **limited scope**:

- ✅ Accepted:
  - Documentation improvements
  - CI/build/tooling improvements
  - Refactors for clarity
  - Non-edge bugfixes (that do not alter strategy rules)

- ❌ Not accepted:
  - Strategy/edge changes (entry/exit rules, thresholds, signal logic)
  - Anything that bypasses or weakens `TRADING_RESTRICTIONS.md`
  - Anything that enables broker-connected paper/live trading

## CLA requirement

A lightweight CLA is required before merging contributions.

Rationale: it allows the maintainer to reuse improvements in private/premium work streams when applicable.

(Implementation note: the maintainer may enable a CLA check/bot for PRs.)

## Ground rules

- Keep PRs small and focused.
- No datasets, keys, or secrets.
- No performance claims.
